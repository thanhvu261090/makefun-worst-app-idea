An entry for the [make.fun bounty "The Worst App Idea Ever Pitched"](https://make.fun/bounty/u-plucky-sparrow-67-the-worst-app-idea-ever-pitched-a0396b23): one sentence, at most 240 characters, pitching the worst and most useless app imaginable.

## The entry

> MoodRouter is a $10-a-month app that asks your Wi-Fi router how it feels twice a day and texts you its mood, so you can finally stop wondering whether your internet is emotionally supported.

**Character count: 190** (limit: 240). One sentence, one idea: a premium subscription that solves a problem nobody has — your router's feelings — with total founder confidence.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) holds the pitch as a single string, gate-checks it — 1 to 240 characters, non-blank, no leading or trailing whitespace, a single line, exactly one sentence ending in a full stop — then writes it to `/output/entry.txt` and prints it.

```sh
python3 main.py
```
